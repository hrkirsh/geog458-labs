# Lab 01: My Digital Geography Workspace

## Place of interest
United States

## Geographic question
How has the spatial distribution of FEMA assistance changed since 2000? 

## Possible data
State boundaries, FEMA public assistance application data, FEMA Grant Manager data

## Mapping idea
Map of median number of FEMA assistance requests by state for 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020, and 2025.
